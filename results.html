Commercial construction supply

From one rep and no CRM to a two-rep team on a system.

2deals closed by the new rep in his first month
100+leads tracked in a CRM that didn’t exist in January
$30Kaverage deal size, with projects that run past $1M

The problem

A bathroom-partition and locker supplier with three install crews and one salesperson who kept his pipeline in his head. No CRM, no follow-up discipline, and 50 to 70 percent of jobs coming from existing customers. The owner wanted a second rep and a way to see what either of them was doing.

What we did

  • Built Close from scratch: pipeline stages, lead qualification, territory rules, and a daily call target
  • Recruited a second rep with a cold-calling background and had him on the phones within weeks
  • Weekly team sales meeting, one-on-one coaching for both reps, owner check-ins
  • Weekly deal review so quotes stop sitting in a drawer

The results

  • The new rep closed two deals in his first month, running his own leads
  • Over 100 leads tracked and worked in the CRM by month three, including a $32K job in play
  • The existing rep went from no CRM to a defined pipeline with follow-up dates on every deal
  • The owner sees both reps’ activity every week instead of asking

Engagement started January 2026. Figures from the weekly sales meetings through March 2026.
