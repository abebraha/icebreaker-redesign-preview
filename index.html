Why we exist

Most sales teams don’t fail. They run out of time.

Every quarter spent hiring and hoping compounds quietly. Not in a dashboard — in missed deals, longer cycles, and a founder still doing demos at nine at night.

Hiring feels like progress. It rarely is. A salesperson without a system is a salary with a countdown on it.

So we don’t sell you a rep, or a playbook, or a consultant. We put a rep on your payroll, build the system around them, and manage them every day until the numbers move. If we can’t help, we say so on the first call.
