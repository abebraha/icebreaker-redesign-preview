Questions

The six things everyone asks.

Who employs the rep?
You do — a W-2 hire on your payroll, with a comp plan we design together. Icebreaker recruits them, builds the system they sell from, and manages them day to day.
Do you make the calls?
No. Your rep does. Our job is to make sure they make enough of them, well: daily activity targets, call reviews, script iteration and the accountability that keeps the numbers honest.
What if the rep quits or underperforms?
In the first 90 days we replace them at no recruiting fee. After that, replacement is part of what you pay us for — we run the process again and the system stays in place while we do.
What kinds of companies is this for?
Owner-led B2B companies selling a service or product with a real sales cycle: logistics, staffing, insurance and benefits, home services, manufacturing, agencies. If your sale closes on a phone call and a follow-up, it fits.
How fast does it start?
Most placements land in four to six weeks. The system is built while we recruit, and monthly billing starts the day your rep does — not the day you sign.
What happens when we outgrow you?
We recruit your full-time sales manager, hand over the playbook over 60 days, and stay available as advisors if you want us. Growing past us is the plan, not a breakup.
