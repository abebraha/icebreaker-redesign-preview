Why we exist

Most sales teams don’t fail. They run out of time.

Every quarter spent hiring and hoping compounds quietly. Not in a dashboard — in missed deals, longer cycles, and a founder still doing demos at nine at night.

Hiring feels like progress. It rarely is. A salesperson without a system is a salary with a countdown on it.

So we don’t sell you a rep, or a playbook, or a consultant. We put a rep on your payroll, build the system around them, and manage them every day until the numbers move. If we can’t help, we say so on the first call.

Your first 90 days

Recruit. Build. Manage.

Three phases. None of them run by you.

Your time: three interviews, then one 30-minute review a week. Ours: every day.

Weeks 1–6 · Recruit

We find the person.

We screen for coachability and work ethic, not charisma. You meet the final two or three and make the call. Nothing is billed monthly until your rep starts.

You getA rep on your payroll, usually within six weeks.
Before day one · Build

We build the system.

How they’re paid, what they say on the phone, where every call gets logged, and the number they have to hit each day. Built while we recruit, so day one is a selling day.

You getA rep who knows exactly what to do on Monday morning.
Every day after · Manage

We run the rep.

We check the numbers every day, coach every week, and walk you through the pipeline every month. If they aren’t working out in the first 90 days, we replace them at no recruiting fee.

You getA Monday email with the numbers, and a rep who keeps getting better.
Accountability

No black box. You see everything.

Calls are one metric. Your business might run on site visits, quotes, demos or LinkedIn conversations. We build a tracker around the four or five activities that actually lead to revenue for you, pull the numbers in automatically, and both of us look at it every day. Nobody gets to hide, including us.
