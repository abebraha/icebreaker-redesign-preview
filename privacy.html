Legal/Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 19, 2026

Icebreaker Sales Development (“Icebreaker,” “we,” “us”) operates icebreakerbd.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you provide. When you contact us, schedule a call, or apply as a candidate, we collect the information you submit, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, work history, compensation preferences, and resume.

Scheduling. Calls are booked through Calendly, which collects your name, email, and selected time under its own privacy policy.

Usage data. If analytics are enabled, we collect standard usage information (pages visited, referring site, device type, approximate location) to understand how the site is used. This data is aggregated and not used to identify you.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to inquiries, evaluate and place sales candidates, provide and improve our services, and communicate with you about our work together. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

Candidate information may be shared with prospective employers as part of the placement process. We use trusted service providers (such as email delivery, scheduling, and hosting vendors) that process data on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email info@icebreakerbd.com and we’ll respond promptly.
