How it works

One rep. One system. Managed every day.

We recruit a salesperson onto your payroll, build everything they need before day one, and run them for you. Here is the whole thing, phase by phase, including what it asks of you.

Your part is small on purpose.

You

  • The rep’s salary. They are your W-2 employee. We design the comp plan with you.

Questions

The ones we get on the first call.

Do you pay the rep’s salary?
No. The rep is your employee and you pay their salary and commission directly. Our fee covers recruiting, the system, and day-to-day management. We design the comp plan with you so both sides make sense.
When does monthly billing start?
The day your rep starts working. If recruiting takes six weeks, you pay nothing monthly for six weeks. The one-time $3,500 recruiting fee is paid up front.
What if the rep doesn’t work out?
If a rep doesn’t meet expectations in the first 90 days, we work the problem with you first. If it’s the wrong person, we replace them at no additional recruiting fee.
Do I need to already have a CRM, a dialer, a script?
No. Setting those up is part of the Build phase. If you already have a CRM we work inside it.
What does the weekly review with you look like?
Thirty minutes. Last week’s numbers against target, the pipeline, what we’re coaching the rep on, and anything we need from you. You get the same in writing every Monday morning.
What happens when we’re ready for an in-house sales manager?
We help you graduate. When the team justifies full-time leadership, we recruit your sales manager for a standard placement fee, transition management over 60 days, and stay available for advisory if you want it.
